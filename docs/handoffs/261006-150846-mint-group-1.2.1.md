- `database is locked` is SQLite giving up after 5 seconds, not two copies of the product by itself. Web plus worker is the normal pair. A second `run-local.ps1` makes it worse.
- The worker holds a database transaction across Slack calls in the older batch executor. mint_group now flushes the group row first, but a lock during that flush fails the job with no Slack post.
- Tests: `.\uv.exe run --with pytest-timeout pytest tests/test_mint_group_jobs.py -q --tb=short --timeout=120`. Full suite is safe.
