## Gotchas & constraints

- Never push/merge directly to `main` without the user; squash-merge PRs.
- On R:, renaming a temp file over existing `sent.json`/`failed.json` can fail with WinError 5 even with Modify rights; in-place writes work. 1.2.4 handles it.
- A mint_group job that fails after posting deletes its posts, but DB group rows committed earlier survive, so later sends "join" a group whose message is gone and fail with `message_not_found`. Fix: retire that group row on EAV, remove the asset from `slack_threads\S<season>.json`, resend.
- Real posts use `<@U...>` mentions resolved by email; test mode posts plain labels on purpose and writes spokes to `slack_threads_test`.
